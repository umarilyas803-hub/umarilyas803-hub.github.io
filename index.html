A CLEAR WAY TO BUILD

Good work starts
with the real problem.

A collaborative process with a clear scope, review points and a working result you can inspect and try.

01 / DISCOVER

Understand the friction

Map who does what today, where time is lost, and what a useful improvement should enable.

02 / SHAPE

Make the flow clear

Agree on the simplest useful scope, user journey, and what counts as done before building.

03 / BUILD

Create a working slice

Build an accessible, responsive experience with clear states and realistic sample content.

04 / VERIFY

Test and hand over

Walk through key scenarios, explain what is live versus a prototype, and document next steps.

QUICK ANSWERS

What clients ask
before we start.

A short, clear guide to what is shown here and how to discuss a project.

Who is Umar Ilyas?

Umar Ilyas is a web developer and AI workflow builder based in Nowshera, Pakistan. He is a computer science graduate building responsive websites, business dashboards, and practical AI workflow prototypes.

What can Umar help me build?

Umar works on responsive websites, web application interfaces, practical workflow prototypes, and early AI or automation concepts. Project scope is agreed after a discovery conversation.

Are the dashboards and product studies connected to live services?

The portfolio demos use sample data. Restaurant AI includes a Node.js project concept; its external WhatsApp and local AI integrations require separate setup and verification. SportsHub and the product studies are presented as interactive previews, not live services.

How do I discuss a project?

Send Umar a short description of the users, workflow, and problem you want to improve through WhatsApp, email, or LinkedIn. He can then suggest a practical first step.
