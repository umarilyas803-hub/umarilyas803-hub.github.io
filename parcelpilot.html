PRODUCT THINKING

Protect the promise
after checkout.

A polished storefront is only half the customer experience. Order visibility and inventory accuracy help a small team deliver what the customer expected.

01 · TRIAGE

See what needs attention

Search the queue and filter by each fulfillment state.

02 · COORDINATE

Keep stock close to orders

Review low-stock items and adjust sample counts alongside the workbench.

03 · FULFILL

Make the next step visible

Prepare a sample shipping step while making clear no carrier action occurred.

WHAT WOULD MAKE THIS REAL

Connect inventory and fulfillment to a real storefront.

A production build would integrate a chosen commerce platform, verified stock sync, carrier rates, returns, and protected access for the shop team.
