# Product presentation, toolkit and project additions

The approved homepage direction now uses a layered native HTML/CSS product presentation for Clinic Desk, Aurelia House and a human-reviewed workflow. Visitors can choose the product focus or pause the showcase. Autoplay pauses offscreen, on hover/focus, when the page is hidden, for reduced-motion preferences and when the global motion setting is paused.

The Tools behind the work section has animated cards and Build, Data, Automation and Delivery filters. Python is labelled Learning & exploration and n8n Workflow exploration; they are not presented as proof of production experience. The toolkit describes actual project use alongside learning areas.

Clinic Desk and Aurelia House are featured projects with dedicated case-study pages. The supplied archives were inspected without modification. Private application source, configuration and data are not republished. The clinic browser walkthrough supports fictional visit selection, completion, sample balance collection, reset and a printable sample slip. The hotel browser walkthrough supports date/guest validation, illustrative room and breakfast totals, request preparation and sample check-in. Both reset on refresh and remain separate from the original PHP/SQLite application and private Cloudflare/D1 workspace.

The homepage contact form prepares a message for WhatsApp or the visitor's email app, with an optional copy action. It never claims automatic delivery. The visitor reviews and sends the message in the chosen app. No third-party submission backend or private message database was introduced.

The review form has animated selection states and the homepage invites genuine feedback. No sample USA/UAE identities, ratings or quotes are published as real reviews. Approved public reviews still come from reviews.json with explicit publication consent.

Aurelia imagery comes from the provided project archive. New clinic and product-presentation images are browser captures of the labelled fictional walkthroughs. Social previews are deterministically typeset over those visuals. New pages have unique metadata, project/WebPage/ImageObject/breadcrumb structured data and image sitemap entries.

Validation covers static integrity, sharing-image references, JSON-LD/JavaScript syntax, email preparation, and Chromium layouts at 320, 390, 768 and 1440 pixels across 15 content pages. Key interactions across the existing nine demos and both new walkthroughs were exercised. Axe WCAG 2 A/AA checks on the changed homepage, reviews and new case studies reported no violations. These checks do not certify full accessibility or validate the archived applications' private backends.
