THREE PERSPECTIVES / ONE USEFUL WEBSITE

What do SEO, AEO and GEO mean?

01 / SEO

Be findable.

Search engine optimization improves crawlability, page structure and relevance so search engines can discover and interpret your pages.

02 / AEO

Be clear.

Answer engine optimization focuses on direct, useful answers to real questions. Clear headings and concise explanations make your expertise easier to understand.

03 / GEO

Be trustworthy.

Generative engine optimization considers how AI search systems discover and reference information. Accurate identity, original evidence and accessible content support that goal.

A PRACTICAL APPROACH

From questions
to useful pages.

A good brief connects your audience, your services and the evidence you can actually show.

  1. 01

    Audit the foundations

    Review crawl access, canonical URLs, titles, internal links, mobile usability and image weight.

  2. 02

    Understand search intent

    Map relevant services and client questions to focused pages. Avoid thin, repetitive country or keyword pages.

  3. 03

    Make the answers useful

    Write clear service scope, process, project examples and FAQs. Keep structured data consistent with visible content.

  4. 04

    Measure and refine

    Review indexed pages, queries, clicks and enquiries using your authorized Search Console or analytics access.

QUESTIONS BEFORE WE START

Useful answers, upfront.

Can you guarantee first-page rankings or AI mentions?

No. Search engines and AI systems decide what to show. The work improves your website’s foundations, clarity and usefulness; it cannot guarantee indexing, rankings, enquiries or citations.

Does AI search need a special file or schema?

Google’s guidance says established SEO practices apply to its AI features, with no special AI text file or special schema required. Important content should be accessible, useful and consistent with structured data.

What access is needed?

A website URL and project goals are enough for an initial public review. Code changes need repository or CMS access. Performance analysis may need read access to Search Console and analytics. Share access through account invitations, not passwords in a form.

Do images and animation improve rankings?

Relevant images can help explain your work. Descriptive alternatives, suitable dimensions and efficient formats support accessibility and page experience. Animation is a design choice, not a ranking guarantee, and should respect reduced-motion preferences.

Can you work with remote clients?

Yes. I am based in Nowshera, Pakistan and work remotely. Scope, communication, timing and deliverables are agreed before a project starts.
