02 / BUILT FOR THE REAL SHIFT

Helpful when the kitchen gets busy.

01

Order with a conversation

WhatsApp text and voice-note flow asks one clear question at a time and confirms customer details before saving.

02

Menu matching with guardrails

Exact names, aliases and fuzzy matches are resolved carefully. Ambiguous items prompt a question; quantities and prices aren't guessed.

03

Staff remains in control

Large orders enter a staff approval window. Valid order statuses, audit history and customer notifications keep a trail.

04

Operational dashboard

Review sales, orders, customers and menu availability. A local AI extractor can fall back to deterministic order parsing when unavailable.
