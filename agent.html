PRODUCT THINKING

Know when to answer.
Know when to ask for help.

A support tool is most useful when it sets clear limits: answer verified FAQs, show the source, and pass ambiguous issues to a person.

01 · ANSWER

Approved information only

Common topics use sample FAQ responses. Unknown or sensitive requests do not receive invented policy answers.

02 · ROUTE

Send uncertainty to review

A customer request appears in the inbox with a category and review state so a teammate can take over.

03 · CLOSE

Keep the status visible

Staff can mark a sample ticket resolved. A real service would record who acted and when.

WHAT WOULD MAKE THIS REAL

Connect approved answers to the tools a team already trusts.

A production build would add a verified knowledge source, secure sign-in, ticket history, service integrations, and human-review rules before any AI answers customers.
