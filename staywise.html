PRODUCT THINKING

A better stay begins
before check-in.

A small hotel team needs guest requests and room readiness in a shared view—without making the experience feel like a chain-hotel portal.

01 · DISCOVER

Book direct with confidence

Show room options, indicative rates, and a simple arrival request on a phone-ready flow.

02 · PREPARE

See the day’s arrivals

Give the front desk a concise sample view of arriving, in-house, and departing guests.

03 · PERSONALIZE

Keep hospitality human

Let software coordinate the routine while staff own guest care and exceptions.

WHAT WOULD MAKE THIS REAL

Connect booking requests to the property’s source of truth.

A production version would integrate the hotel’s reservation system, live room inventory, verified rates, guest consent, and secure staff roles.
